# Resume evidence and ATS comparison

Compare the job description's stated responsibilities and qualifications with
evidence explicitly present in the resume. Distinguish a demonstrated skill from
a keyword that appears without supporting experience. A skill absent from the
resume is not proof that the candidate lacks that skill; describe it as an
unobserved or not-evidenced requirement.

An ATS-style match assessment should reflect the relevance and evidence of
required skills, responsibilities, experience, and qualifications in this
specific resume and job description. It is an estimate, not a guarantee of how
an employer's applicant-tracking system will score an application.

Resume suggestions should be actionable and tied to an actual role requirement
and an actual resume detail. Recommend clearer wording, measurable outcomes, or
reordering only when the provided resume gives evidence to support the change.
Never invent an employer, qualification, project, metric, or achievement.

When a job description uses terminology not present in a resume, consider
whether the resume describes an equivalent responsibility or skill using
different words before identifying a gap.
