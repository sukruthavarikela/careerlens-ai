# Job skills and role comparison

Job descriptions commonly express skills as technical capabilities, tools,
domain knowledge, collaboration behaviors, and responsibilities. Compare each
requirement to concrete resume evidence rather than assuming proficiency from
job titles alone.

Separate requirements explicitly called required or essential from preferred
qualifications. A useful skill-gap explanation identifies the requirement,
whether the resume contains direct or adjacent evidence, and what evidence would
make the match clearer. Do not claim a candidate lacks a skill when the resume
simply does not mention it.

Relevant job-role recommendations should be supported by the candidate's
documented responsibilities, skills, and experience, with the supplied job
description providing role context. Do not infer seniority, credentials, or
career history that the resume does not establish.
